# Statement for the Public Record: A Falsifiable Prediction Regarding the Pending Federal Definition of Ultra-Processed Foods

**Submitted to:** Docket FDA-2025-N-1793 (Request for Information: Ultra-Processed Foods; 90 FR 35305) — for the record, post-comment-period; and Docket FDA-2025-N-3262 (Substances Generally Recognized as Safe; proposed rule of Aug. 11, 2026), as relevant to the data fields of the proposed notification system.
**Author:** Paul Murff, Editor, Biology as Code — an open (Apache-2.0), DOI-registered standards effort for typed nutrition data (reference implementation: github.com/murffious/biology_as_code; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21536449). The author operates no commercial implementation of these standards and holds no financial interest in any food, ingredient, or scoring product.
**Date:** August 2026. Simultaneously archived with DOI on Zenodo to establish timestamp.

## 1. Purpose

On August 10, 2026, HHS and USDA submitted the federal government's first proposed
definition of ultra-processed foods for final review. This statement places on the
public record, in advance of that definition's publication, a specific and falsifiable
prediction about its performance — together with the technical alternative that would
change the predicted outcome. The intent is scientific: to make the definitional
choice testable, whichever form it takes.

## 2. The prediction

**P1.** If the final federal definition of "ultra-processed food" is expressible as an
ingredient-marker or analyte-threshold checklist — a test that classifies products by
the presence of listed additive categories and/or nutrient levels — then, within
approximately one product-reformulation cycle (24–36 months of the definition
acquiring regulatory or major commercial consequence), a substantial share of affected
products will be reformulated to exit the definition **without material change to the
properties the definition exists to address**: degree of matrix transformation, energy
density, eating rate, and palatability engineering.

**P2.** If, instead, the definition is anchored in **method identity** (the recorded
sequence of physical and chemical transformations applied) and **matrix state** (the
measurable structural result — e.g., particle/cell-integrity class, physical form),
reformulation-to-exit will not occur at scale, because exiting such a definition
requires actually changing what was done to the food.

**Falsification criteria.** P1 is falsified if, three years after an
ingredient/analyte-style definition takes effect, market analyses show no significant
category migration out of the definition via marker-substitution (removal or swap of
trigger additives) among top-selling affected SKUs. P1 is confirmed if such migration
is observed while independent measures of transformation degree, energy density, and
hyper-palatability profile in the affected categories remain substantially unchanged.
The author commits to publishing the evaluation either way.

## 3. Basis

The predicted behavior is the documented historical response to every prior
operational target in U.S. food regulation. The 2006 trans-fat label line produced a
reformulation exodus years before the 2015 GRAS revocation. The EU's 2010 warning
requirement on six azo dyes produced continent-wide voluntary reformulation to
avoid the label. "Clean label" demand produced marker-additive removal without
structural change. The absence of any federal definition of "net carbs" produced a
privately defined checklist and a product category engineered to it. Chile's
threshold-triggered warning octagons — the strongest package implemented to date —
produced measurable purchase shifts *and* reformulation to non-listed sweeteners,
because its triggers are analyte thresholds. Reformulation-to-the-test is not
misconduct; it is the rational and invariant market response to any test expressible
at the ingredient/analyte layer. The scientific literature independently supports the
mechanism: cohort analyses of NOVA-classified subgroups diverge in direction (e.g.,
sweetened beverages and processed meats adverse; ultra-processed whole-grain breads
and cereals null-to-inverse), and a 2025 systematic review concludes that causal
effects "cannot be identified" at the category level — because the category is a
lossy compression of separable variables.

## 4. The constructive alternative

The durable regulatory object is not a category but a set of typed variables, with
"ultra-processed" retained, if desired, as a computed region over them:

1. **Method identity** — the ordered transformation operations applied, recorded as a
   data field (the analog, for processing, of the ingredient statement);
2. **Matrix state** — the structural result, in measurable classes;
3. **Formulation/reward profile** — additive functional purpose and
   hyper-palatability indicators, the latter computable today from existing
   Nutrition Facts data.

Regulatory instruments then attach to **thresholds on axes** — the established
mechanism of the nutrient panel — rather than to membership in a class. Processing
history functions as the *prior*; measured or modeled physiological response
functions as the *posterior* and final standard. Under this architecture, the
definition cannot be exited by marker substitution, and the definitional dispute
currently dividing the scientific community (whether "the processing" or "the
nutrients" carry the harm) becomes an answerable, measurable interaction rather than
a permanent controversy.

**Specific request regarding Docket FDA-2025-N-3262:** the proposed mandatory GRAS
notification system will create the federal government's first ingredient inventory.
The notification schema should carry, at minimum, method-of-production identity and
intended functional purpose for each substance — fields without which the inventory
will record *what* enters the food supply while remaining blind to *what was done*
to produce and deploy it. An open reference schema (FDP-1, Food Data Provenance
Declaration, Apache-2.0, Zenodo-archived) is available for incorporation or
adaptation without restriction.

## 5. Standing of this statement

This statement is offered by an independent, open-standards effort with no commercial
position. Its purpose is to ensure that, whichever definitional path the agencies
choose, the choice is evaluated against a prediction made before the outcome was
known. Definitions that can be tested improve; definitions that cannot be tested
persist. The 1941 standard of nutritional adequacy persisted, untested, for
eighty-five years. The present definition deserves a better fate, and this record
is intended to help provide one.
