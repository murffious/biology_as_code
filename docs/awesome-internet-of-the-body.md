# Awesome Internet of the Body

> The curated list of apps and platforms that **gather human data** — the "Internet
> of the Body" (IoB) — now lives in its **own repository**.

## ➡️ [murffious/awesome-internet-of-the-body](https://github.com/murffious/awesome-internet-of-the-body)

The **Internet of the Body** (IoB) is the growing web of wearables, implants,
sensors, and apps that measure a human being and move that data across a network.
The list catalogs where you can **consume that human data via app integrations** —
an API, SDK, webhook, or open export — organized by category, with an
open-source-first bias and a `live` / `snapshot` cadence tag on each data feed.

It's the natural **sensor-layer companion** to `biology-as-code`: this project
models what a body *does* with its inputs; the Internet of the Body is where the
real signals would come from. The same ethos applies in both — **empty beats
fake**, provenance is mandatory, and missing data is `UNEVALUABLE`, not a green
light.
